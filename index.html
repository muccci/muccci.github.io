About

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Guoxing Chen. I am part of the Network Security and Privacy Protection (NSEC) Lab. Before joining SJTU, I received my B.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology.

My research is centered on confidential computing and Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs), including related topics such as memory encryption and side-channel issues in TEE settings.
